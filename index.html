About me.

Hi, I'm Janu Ahirwar, a B.Tech Mechanical Engineering student at University Institute of Technology, RGPV, Shivpuri, Madhya Pradesh. My interests lie at the intersection of Mechanical Engineering, Design, Technology, Data Analytics, and AI.

I have completed the Mastercard Advisors & Consulting Services Virtual Experience, gaining exposure to professional problem-solving and consulting-based work. My recent project, MediStock-AI, is an AI-assisted healthcare inventory and supply-chain solution that uses data and machine learning to forecast demand, identify stock-out risks, and support inventory replenishment decisions.

I'm continuously learning, building, and experimenting with new technologies, with the goal of combining my engineering foundation with modern technology to develop practical solutions for real-world problems.

MediStock-AI

AI-Assisted Healthcare Inventory & Supply Chain Management System

An AI-assisted inventory solution that helps healthcare facilities make better inventory and replenishment decisions. It analyzes historical inventory data to forecast short-term demand, monitor stock levels, calculate days of stock cover, identify potential stock-out risks, and suggest replenishment quantities. Built with a Random Forest model for demand forecasting; the dashboard turns inventory data into clear insights for timely reorders.

Miniature Wind Turbine

Off-Grid Power Generation | B.Tech Mechanical Engineering

Designed and fabricated a portable Horizontal Axis Wind Turbine (HAWT) for low-wind conditions, focusing on mechanical design, power generation, and performance testing. The turbine achieved a cut-in speed of approximately 2.5–3 m/s and generated ~7–8 V at ~955 RPM, demonstrating its potential for small-scale off-grid power applications.
